# FitVault

**The Obsidian Training Log & Program Vault.** A plain-Markdown vault for
logging your training in Obsidian — no plugins required.

- **18 plain-Markdown files** — no code, no scripts, no CSV; opens in any editor.
- **Session log** with fixed columns (slot, exercise, sets x reps, load, RPE, note).
- **Exercise cards** with 1RM and a training max (90% of 1RM, rounded to 2.5 kg).
- **Block and 5-3/1 week tracking**, plus a scheduled deload week.
- **PR records with Epley estimates**: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30).
- **Cardio, body metrics, and dashboards** (a filled fictional worked example
  plus a blank shell).
- Dataview is optional — everything above works with vanilla markdown.
