# FitVault

A plain-Markdown Obsidian vault for logging training: 18 Markdown files, no
code, no scripts, no CSV, and no plugins required (Dataview is optional if you
want rolled-up views). It structures a training block — a session log with
identical columns every time (slot, exercise, sets x reps, load, RPE, note),
one card per lift with a 1RM and a training max (90 percent, rounded to 2.5),
block and 5-3-1 week tracking, PR records with Epley estimates (weight ×
(1 + reps ÷ 30)), cardio, body metrics, a deload week, and dashboards
(one filled fictional worked example plus a blank shell).

**Honest note:** this is a log, not a coach. Worked examples are fictional.
